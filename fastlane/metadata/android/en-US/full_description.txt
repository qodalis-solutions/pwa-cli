Qodalis CLI puts a real command line on your phone. Type a command, press Enter: more than 100 commands run right on your device, work offline and need no online account.

EVERYDAY TOOLS
• guid, hash (SHA-1/256/384/512), base64, hex, url, jwt decode, binary and morse
• generate-password for strong random passwords
• qr draws QR codes right in the terminal
• Charts and Markdown rendered in the terminal
• regex, json and csv tools, plus string tools: upper, camelCase, slug, pad, replace and more
• cal, timestamps, cron schedules, lorem ipsum and random numbers
• A to-do list, a stopwatch and a countdown timer
• curl and wget for HTTP requests, speed-test for your connection

FILES AND THE NANO EDITOR
• ls, cd, mkdir, cat, cp, mv, rm, tree, find, grep, sed, awk, sort, head, tail and wc
• Edit any file with the built-in nano editor
• Pipes, aliases, environment variables, history search and Tab completion
• Your files stay on your device between sessions

GAMES, AND MORE WITH PKG
• 2048, Tetris, Snake, Minesweeper, Sudoku and Wordle, played by touch or keyboard
• pkg browse lists extra packages and pkg add installs one in seconds
• Connect Four, Hangman, 15-puzzle and Mastermind are on the way

YOUR PHONE FROM THE COMMAND LINE
• device, battery and network show what your phone is up to
• share sends the output of any command to another app; vibrate and toast for your scripts

MADE FOR TOUCH SCREENS
• A key row with Esc, Tab, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+L, arrows and the symbols phone keyboards hide
• Typing without autocorrect, so commands arrive exactly as typed
• A short tour on first launch shows you around
• Themes, adjustable font size and 11 languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian and Chinese

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
Your account, files and to-dos are stored only on your device. The app goes online only for commands that need it, such as curl or speed-test, and to show ads. Anonymous usage statistics help improve the app; turn them off with analytics off.

Qodalis CLI is free, open source and built on the Qodalis web terminal framework: https://cli.qodalis.com
